Process

From data to action in seconds

Five stages, running continuously, turning raw signals into a warning someone can act on.

01

Collect

Rainfall systems, IoT sensors, satellites, GIS layers and historical records feed the pipeline.

02

Analyse

AI and ML models read the environmental and geographical patterns behind the raw numbers.

03

Predict

The system scores each zone and flags where a landslide is becoming likely.

04

Alert

Warnings reach authorities and communities in real time, in their own language.

05

Respond

Emergency teams prioritise critical areas and coordinate the response on the ground.
